Gingerbread Pancakes

Festive Vibe: Warm spices, holiday morning classic

Serves: 2–3

Ingredients
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground cloves
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 cup buttermilk (or milk + 1 tsp vinegar)
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Maple syrup (for serving)
Instructions
  1. Whisk flour, brown sugar, baking powder, baking soda, spices, and salt.
  2. Mix buttermilk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla in another bowl.
  3. Pour wet into dry and stir gently until just combined.
  4. Heat a greased nonstick skillet over medium heat.
  5. Portion ¼ cup batter per pancake.
  6. Cook until bubbles form, flip, and cook until golden.
  7. Serve warm with maple syrup and a sprinkle of powdered sugar.
🍊 Cranberry Orange Baked Oatmeal

Festive Vibe: Bright, cozy, and perfect for sharing

Serves: 4–6

Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • ½ cup fresh or dried cranberries
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1½ cups milk
  • ¼ cup maple syrup or honey
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter or coconut oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) and grease a baking dish.
  2. In a bowl, combine oats, cranberries, orange zest, cinnamon, nutmeg, baking powder, and salt.
  3. In another bowl, whisk milk, maple syrup, egg, butter, and vanilla.
  4. Pour wet mixture over dry ingredients and stir to combine.
  5. Transfer mixture to prepared baking dish.
  6. Bake for 35–40 minutes until set and lightly golden.
  7. Let cool slightly and serve with yogurt or extra maple syrup.
🍞 Eggnog French Toast

Festive Vibe: Rich, indulgent, holiday brunch favorite

Serves: 2–3

Ingredients
  • 6 slices thick bread (brioche or challah)
  • ¾ cup eggnog
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Butter for cooking
  • Powdered sugar (optional)
Instructions
  1. In a shallow bowl, whisk together eggnog, eggs, cinnamon, nutmeg, and vanilla.
  2. Heat a skillet over medium heat and melt butter.
  3. Dip each slice of bread into the eggnog mixture, coating both sides.
  4. Place bread onto skillet and cook until golden brown, about 2–3 minutes per side.
  5. Repeat with remaining slices, adding more butter as needed.
  6. Serve warm with powdered sugar, maple syrup, or fresh berries.
