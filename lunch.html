🎄 Festive Herb-Roasted Turkey
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1 whole turkey (10–12 lbs), thawed
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 cups chicken or turkey broth
  • Optional: carrots, celery, and onion for roasting pan
👩‍🍳 Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C). Pat the turkey dry with paper towels.
  2. In a small bowl, mix butter, garlic, rosemary, thyme, sage, salt, and pepper.
  3. Gently loosen the skin over the turkey breast and rub half of the butter mixture underneath the skin.
  4. Rub the remaining butter all over the outside of the turkey.
  5. Stuff the turkey cavity with lemon halves and onion quarters.
  6. Place turkey on a roasting rack in a pan. Add broth and optional vegetables to the bottom of the pan.
  7. Roast uncovered, basting occasionally, for 2½–3 hours, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
  8. Remove turkey from oven and let rest for 20 minutes before carving.
✨ Serving Tip: Garnish with fresh herbs, cranberry sauce, and roasted vegetables for a festive presentation.
