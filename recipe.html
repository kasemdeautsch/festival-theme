🎄 Festive Herb-Roasted Turkey
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1 whole turkey (10–12 lbs), thawed
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 cups chicken or turkey broth
  • Optional: carrots, celery, and onion for roasting pan
👩‍🍳 Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C). Pat the turkey dry with paper towels.
  2. In a small bowl, mix butter, garlic, rosemary, thyme, sage, salt, and pepper.
  3. Gently loosen the skin over the turkey breast and rub half of the butter mixture underneath the skin.
  4. Rub the remaining butter all over the outside of the turkey.
  5. Stuff the turkey cavity with lemon halves and onion quarters.
  6. Place turkey on a roasting rack in a pan. Add broth and optional vegetables to the bottom of the pan.
  7. Roast uncovered, basting occasionally, for 2½–3 hours, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
  8. Remove turkey from oven and let rest for 20 minutes before carving.
✨ Serving Tip: Garnish with fresh herbs, cranberry sauce, and roasted vegetables for a festive presentation.
Gingerbread Pancakes

Festive Vibe: Warm spices, holiday morning classic

Serves: 2–3

Ingredients
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground cloves
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 cup buttermilk (or milk + 1 tsp vinegar)
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Maple syrup (for serving)
Instructions
  1. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, brown sugar, baking powder, baking soda, spices, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, mix buttermilk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla.
  3. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and gently stir until just combined.
  4. Heat a nonstick skillet over medium heat and lightly grease it.
  5. Pour ¼ cup batter for each pancake onto the skillet.
  6. Cook until bubbles form, then flip and cook until golden.
  7. Serve warm with maple syrup and a sprinkle of powdered sugar.
🍊 Cranberry Orange Baked Oatmeal

Festive Vibe: Bright, cozy, and perfect for sharing

Serves: 4–6

Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • ½ cup fresh or dried cranberries
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1½ cups milk
  • ¼ cup maple syrup or honey
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter or coconut oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) and grease a baking dish.
  2. In a bowl, combine oats, cranberries, orange zest, cinnamon, nutmeg, baking powder, and salt.
  3. In another bowl, whisk milk, maple syrup, egg, butter, and vanilla.
  4. Pour wet mixture over dry ingredients and stir to combine.
  5. Transfer mixture to prepared baking dish.
  6. Bake for 35–40 minutes until set and lightly golden.
  7. Let cool slightly and serve with yogurt or extra maple syrup.
🍞 Eggnog French Toast

Festive Vibe: Rich, indulgent, holiday brunch favorite

Serves: 2–3

Ingredients
  • 6 slices thick bread (brioche or challah)
  • ¾ cup eggnog
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Butter for cooking
  • Powdered sugar (optional)
Instructions
  1. In a shallow bowl, whisk together eggnog, eggs, cinnamon, nutmeg, and vanilla.
  2. Heat a skillet over medium heat and melt butter.
  3. Dip each slice of bread into the eggnog mixture, coating both sides.
  4. Place bread onto skillet and cook until golden brown, about 2–3 minutes per side.
  5. Repeat with remaining slices, adding more butter as needed.
  6. Serve warm with powdered sugar, maple syrup, or fresh berries.
